## Gig title
I will audit and fix your Google Ads account to cut wasted spend

## Category
Digital Marketing > Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

## Description
Is your Google Ads account spending more than it earns? I turn accounts around.

I am a senior performance marketing contractor with deep experience in Google Ads, GA4, Value-Based Bidding, BigQuery and Looker Studio. I also work in regulated sectors, so I know the rules around ad copy for health and similar industries (ASA, MHRA, GPhC).

What you get: a clear read of where your money is going, what is broken in tracking and conversion setup, and a plain list of fixes in priority order.

What I look at: campaign structure, conversion tracking and GA4 setup, bidding strategy, search terms and wasted spend, ad copy and landing pages, and reporting.

How it works: you give me read access to your Google Ads and GA4. I send you the findings and the fix list. If you want, I make the changes too.
